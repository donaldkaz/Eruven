Last time the party traveled 4 days south east of Blackwell headed towards the nearest port city of Harnmouth. They were chasing down the Gilded Hand who had kidnapped Kit Kat's twin sister Clem. They took a shortcut through the Elgenwode, spending one night there and being ambushed by an Ankeg creature on the first day. At the end of the day that the party emerged from the wode they came upon the Gilded Hand's camp as night fell. They sprung a successful attack and now have the Gilded Hand at their mercy.

Regardless of if the Hand is killed or not, the party will save Clem, who will be initially angry at Kit Kit. The party will also either learn from the Hand or from correspondence they find that they were in league with Queen Bargnot of the Jagged Edge. They were in possession of a magical ring required to unseal the third level of the Delian Tomb's level.

When the party returns to Blackwell, they will come to the aftermath of the Wraith Raid.
