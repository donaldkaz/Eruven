The [[The Fey Courts#Archfey|Archfey]] Ryll's domains include **Indulgence, Mischief, Overindulgence, and Ruinous Delight**. He believes that everything good in this [[The Mundane World of Eruven|world]] will go away eventually, and as such one should do all they can to enjoy life to its fullest extent. He goes by a variety of different titles, but chiefly amongst them is The Prince of Sweet Things.

He resides in an unknown [[The Fey Courts|Fey Court]] devoted to pleasure, excess, and whimsy. His court is wildly colorful and flamboyant, with most everything made of some kind of sugar. Nothing sweet comes without a price, however, just as teeth will rot if one indulges too much in sweet and sugary things, a person who becomes trapped by the sugary sweetness of Ryll and his Court may find themselves paying dire consequences. His court is said to be a place where the rivers run full with nectar and syrup, the forests and plants grow candied fruits, and the air is heavy with the smells of ripe fruit and fresh candies.

Ryll is not evil in any true sense of the word, but he is profoundly unrestrained and chaotic. He believes: desire is truth, pleasure is sacred, and sweetness only matters because one day it will rot and fade. He despises those who live ascetic lives or those who ascribe to joyless moralities and he hates those who would refuse a gift.
