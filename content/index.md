***A Foreword by [[Alvare Calbo]], Dean of the Scrivere School, Imperial University***

Within the following pages lies the culmination of my lifetime’s devotion to the diligent study and faithful scribing of all that may be known in our world of [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]]. I have journeyed to every corner of this vast realm, and have often placed myself in great peril to compile what I believe to be the most comprehensive collections of  observations and knowledge ever assembled, now presented here as *The Encyclopedia of Eruven*.

Fortune has smiled generously upon my endeavors, I have been blessed with friends and colleagues possessed of saintly patience, and with a family whose forbearance during my many months abroad has been a source of constant strength. I owe particular gratitude to my patron, His Grace [[Duke Angiolo Vedero]], whose beneficence has secured for me a permanent study within the Imperial University and whose appointment of me as Dean of the [[Scrivere School]] has enabled and sustained my scholarly pursuits.

It is my earnest hope that the knowledge so painstakingly gathered herein may serve you well, and that you too may walk in the fortune you forge.
