Unique among our world's prominent [[Species & Cultures|species]], the term elf refers to one of the the multiple different intelligent humanoid peoples created by the [[The Celestials|Celestials]], who were themselves created by the [[The Elder Creator Gods|Elder God]] [[Tel]]. 

The different elven sub-ancestries have many differences, due in large part to the differences between the different celestials who made them. Although they share many differences, the also have a few things in common, mainly basic physical characteristics. All elves' height ranges from 5.5 to about 6.5 feet, weighing about 100 to 150 pounds, and can expect to live from around 400 to 600 years. In addition to these shared physical characteristics, all elves share a common connection to the old magic of the [[The Mundane World of Eruven|world]], and they all share a deep appreciation and connection with art, music, and poetry.

For more information specific to the different kinds of elves, refer to the below list:
- [[High Elves]] children of the [[Solis Celestials]].
- [[Twilight Elves]] children of both the [[Solis Celestials|Solis]] and [[Dusk Celestials|Dusk]] Celestials.
- [[Wode Elves]] children of the [[Dusk Celestials]].
