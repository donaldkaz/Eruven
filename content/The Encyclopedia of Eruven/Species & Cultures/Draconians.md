Draconians, sometimes known as Dragonborn, Wyrmwrights, or Dragon Knights, are a [[Species & Cultures|people]] created through an extremely difficult and obscure ritual known as Dracogenisis. Draconians are muscled humanoids who closely resemble dragons, most of which have an elemental affinity connected to their scales.

Draconians are known across [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]] as highly honorable and fiercely loyal warriors. The largest population of draconians in Eruven is the remnants of the [[Dragon Knights]], created by [[King Aldrin the Just's]] closest advisor and court wizard [[Solomon Ebeus]]. The Dragon Knights once numbered several thousand of the king's greatest knight. Knighthood was a title carried by every member of that first generation of dragon knights. For over 30 years, these knights were symbols of law and order, protecting the weak from the strong, and standing between the common folk and those who sought power over others. Then, [[Thalric, King Beneath the Umbral Sun]] betrayed the late Kind Aldrin and the once proud dragon knights had been outlawed and hunted.
# Cultures
Draconians do not have the population to have their own distinct societies or cultures, instead they are found, in small numbers, imbedded within the various [[Human Realms|human realms]] of Eruven. There are small populations of draconians in [[Nephara]], [[Angorath]], and [[Zal'amar]], with the largest extant population in [[Corvallis]] known as the Dragon Knights, being the remnants of the late Kind Aldrin's elite company of knights.
In modern times, the dragon knights of Corvallis are forced to live in hiding, due to their order being disbanded and outlawed under the rule of [[Thalric, King Beneath the Umbral Sun|The Crown Imperishable]], with an enormous bounty placed on the head of every surviving knight.
# Origins
Draconians are created by the esoteric arcane ritual of Dracogenisis, a ritual which is extremely difficult for even the most experienced wizard to master. The small populations in Nephara, Angorath, and Zal'amar attest to this. Descendants of original generations created millennia ago by powerful wizards, they have never been numerous. A typical clutch yields only a single egg, after only a few generations, these draconians begin to show new adaptations like feathers or frilled ridges.
# Appearance
Draconians stand tall, with their height typically ranging from 6 to 7 feet, weighing between 200 and 350 pounds. Draconians often have shorter lifespans than an equivalent human, expecting to live anywhere between 50 and 90 years. Draconians sport naturally muscular frames, and their reptilian bodies are covered in dragon-like scales with colors that span the rainbow. All draconians have wings, but only a few have wings large enough to actually let them fly.
# Religion
*For more on this subject, see [[The Successor Gods]]*

Draconian practice the [[Religion & Deities|Religions]] of whichever culture or society they had been raised in. Because the most populous group of draconians are the dragon knights of Corvallis, most draconians will worship the [[Human Gods of Corvallis]].
# Homelands
Draconians have no true homeland due to the nature of their fabricated origins, instead, draconians call whichever culture they were raised in as their home.

There are small groups of draconians in [[Nephara]], [[Angorath]], and [[Zal'amar]]. There is a larger group of draconians known as the Dragon Knights who call [[Corvallis]] their home.
