The Kaynak [[Species & Cultures|people]] are [[Giantkin]] and the descendants of a tribe of [[Giants]] from [[Zhaern]] who could see the moment and nature of their own deaths. They are extremely large humanoids with a friendly and outgoing nature.

They are a pastoral people, known for their impressive masonry and stonework. However, in spite of their friendly nature, the rare presence of a kaynak in human society is consider a harbinger, an omen of dark times. The kaynak peoples have prophetic visions of their own deaths known as Doomsight, which is often a cause for a kaynak to travel out into the world and interact with outside societies and cultures.
# Cultures
The kaynak are the descendants of a tribe of giants in northern [[Zhaern]], the original Kay'nak tribe traded some of their  size and strength for the ability to see the future with a [[The Gods|god]]. Although they are descended from stone giants they do not consider themselves to be giants. Kaynak tribes lead lives deeply connected to the natural world, working as shepherds, masons, and farmers.

The only kaynak the average human meets is one trying to fulfill their Doomsight. The human superstition, that the arrival of one or more kaynak in human lands is a sign of great forces acting in the world, is literally true. In dark times, many kaynak experience the Doomsight and leave their communities to venture out into the [[The Mundane World of Eruven|mundane world]], in search of their destiny.
# Origins
The kaynak's ancestors made a bargain with[[Holkatya]] the [[Human Gods of Zhaern|Zhaernic]] trickster god. They traded some of their gigantic size and strength for the ability to see the future.

But Holkatya betrayed them, and the only future a kaynak is allowed to see is the moment and nature of their own death. These visions are never of some mundane tragedy. No kaynak has ever received a vision of dying from choking on a grape. This Doomsight is always momentous, always dramatic.

The resulting descendants of these stone giants who made this deal with Holkatya became the kaynak tribes that live in the foothills across Eruven.
# Appearance
Kaynak stand at imposing heights, ranging from 9 to 10 feet tall, and can weigh anywhere from 1,000 to 2,000 pounds due to the fact that their well-muscled bodies are made of organic stone. Kaynak can often expect to live for 100 to 200 years. Kaynak skin shades spans the colors of the rocks their flesh is made of, including sandstone, marble, granite, and basalt, and Kaynak have dark black hair.
# Religion
*For more on this subject, see [[Kaynak Heroes]]*

Most kaynak in Eruven are animists. They know and respect the four [[The Elder Creator Gods|Elder Gods]] who created the world and believe these gods watch the world and see what happens within it. However, the kaynak do not create churches or formal belief systems around this attitude. Instead, they believe the world is filled with innumerable nature spirits, each river, tree, stone has their own spirit, which the kaynak revere. 

Kaynak shamans and theologians venerate kaynak [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|heroes]] who, after doing great deeds in life, were chosen by the gods to take their place among the stars, becoming constellations. They see the stars at night as a complex map of legendary [[Kaynak Heroes|kaynak heroes]], and their own ancestors who watch from above.
# Homelands
Kaynak originally hail from the foothills of the [[The Bald Mountains|Bald Mountains]] in northern [[Zhaern]]. In modern times, kaynak tribes can be found hidden in the foothills of most mountain chains across Eruven.
