The Kaynak [[Species & Cultures|people]] are [[Giantkin]] and the descendants of a tribe of [[Giants]] from [[Zhaern]] who could see the moment and nature of their own deaths. They are extremely large humanoids with a friendly and outgoing nature.

In spite of their friendly nature, the rare presence of a kaynak in human society is consider a harbinger, an omen of dark times. They are a pastoral people, known for their impressive masonry and stonework.
# Cultures
The kaynak are the descendants of a tribe of giants in northern Zhaern, the original Kay'nak tribe traded some of their gigantic size and strength for the ability to see the future with a [[The Gods|god]]. Although they are descended from [[Stone Giants|stone giants]] they do not consider themselves giants. Kaynak culture live lives connected to the natural world, living as shepherds, masons, and farmers.
# Origins
The kaynak's ancestors traded a portion of their gigantic size and strength to see the future with [[Holkatya]], the [[Human Gods of Zhaern|Zhaernic]] trickster god.
# Appearance
Height 9 to 10 feet. Weight 1,000-2,000 pounds. Life expectancy 100-200 years.
