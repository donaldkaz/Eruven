Devils and Tieflings are a native [[Species & Cultures|species]] of the [[The Seven Cities of Hell|Seven Cities of Hell]]. Devils who join "the trade", as their civil service is called, spend their days in bureaucratic service hoping or scheming for promotion. Devils looking for a quick path up the bureaucratic ladder sign up for the [[The Exchange|Exchange]], whereby mortals in the [[The Mundane World of Eruven|mundane world]] who perform the right rituals can summon a devil, who bargains with the supplicant on behalf of their [[The Lords of Hell|archdevil]]. Archdevils can grant temporary worldly power in exchange for a supplicant's soul, with the summoned devil acting as the broker. On rare occasions, the summoning goes wrong and the supplicant dies before a deal can be struck, stranding the summoned devil on Eruven permanently. Some stranded devils seek to return to Hell, but most prefer life in Eruven, where the phrase "stabbed in the back by a colleague" is usually a metaphor.

The majority of devils in Eruven are not from, nor have ever been to, the Seven Cities. They are descendants of devils who were stranded in the mundane world decades, centuries, or even millennia ago. These descendant of native born devils are known as tieflings, and their fiendish features and traits can oftentimes skip entire generations only to reappear in an unlucky child.
# Cultures
*For more on the subject of culture and society in Hell, see [[The Seven Cities of Hell]]*

In Eruven, devils and tieflings typically do not have their own distinct cultures or societies, rather, they oftentimes integrate themselves within the various [[Human Realms|human realms]]. Native born devils can find themselves easily finding work as bookkeepers, administrators, and traders in metropolitan and rural cities alike, with their training and work within the Exchange in Hell serving as excellent preparation for numbers oriented and cutthroat businesses. Devils and tieflings often face trouble being accepted by many more rural societies due to their appearance and the somewhat faulty assumption of the innate evil nature of devils. Because of their trouble with integrating into rural communities, devils and tieflings will be much more commonly encountered in the bustling streets of the largest human cities.
# Origins
Devils are native to the [[The Lower Planes of Existence|Lower Plane]] of Hell, also known as the last plane of law. It is theorized that all devils were created in the image of the Archdevils of Hell to serve as their loyal bureaucrats, warriors, and secretaries.

Tieflings, on the other hand, are the descendants of devils stranded on Eruven or the offspring of humans who have had significant interaction with devils or the Lower Plane of Hell. A family who may have had a devil in their heritage very far in the past may one day have the fiendish features of their devil ancestor reappear in a child, resulting in a surprise tiefling baby.
# Appearance
Devils and Tieflings share much of their base physical attributes with humans, their height typically ranging between 5 to 6 feet, weighing 120 to 250 pounds, and can expect to live for 70 to 120 years. Their skin is often red or blue, expressed in a wide variety of hues, from bright crimson to deep purple. Each devil or tiefling is born with at least one *hellmark:* horns, a tail, glowing eyes, cloven hooves, a forked tongue, fanged incisors, or even wings.
# Religion
*For more on this subject, see either [[The Successor Gods]] or [[The Lords of Hell]]*

Tieflings in Eruven usually serve the [[The Gods|gods]] and [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|saints]] of the culture in which they were raised.

Devils native to the [[The Seven Cities of Hell|Seven Cities]] have saints like humans of Eruven do but in place of gods, these saints serve the seven [[The Lords of Hell|Archdukes of Hell]], also known as Archdevils or the Lords of Hell. [[Religion & Deities|Religion]] in Hell is superficially similar to religion among the peoples of Eruven. There are churches and rites and rituals, but devils tend to view attending church and performing the expected rituals at the appropriate times as akin to paying taxes.
# Homelands
Devils are from the Seven Cities of Hell and the stranded devils and tieflings found on the mundane plane hail from the many human realms.
