Fey are creatures from [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven's]] ancient [[Wodes|wodes]] and are originally native to the two [[The Planar Expanse|moons]] of [[Tel]]: [[Lux the Moon of Dawn]] and [[Nox the Moon of Dusk]]. Fey creatures are deeply tied to the old magics of the world and are extremely capricious creatures.

Fey are strongly associated with the natural areas of Eruven and are most likely to be found in the elf-haunted wodes, especially in areas where there is a weak barrier in the [[The Astral Sea|Astral Sea]] between the Mundane World and either of the two moons.
# Members
There are many different species of fey, and they frustratingly often have extremely little in common aside from being originally native to the Fey Moons. A few common threads can be drawn between the many kinds of fey depending on the Fey Moon of origin. The Moon of Dawn and Dusk are like two sides of a coin, much like tragedy and comedy in drama. For example, creatures like pixies, dryads, goblins, and fauns trace their origin to the [[Lux the Moon of Dawn|Moon of Dawn]] and creatures like hags, lycanthropes, owlbears, and trolls trace their origin to the [[Nox the Moon of Dusk|Moon of Dusk]]. # Cultures
There is no united fey culture, as most fey creatures do not actually associate with each other solely along the lines of their shared origins. With that said, scattered throughout the various [[Wodes|wodes]] of Eruven are [[The Fey Courts|Fey Courts]]; collections of fey creatures lead by demigod-like beings known as [[The Fey Courts#Archfey|Archfey]]. These courts do not function like any kind of distinct nation, however they are groups of different fey creatures with similar ideological leanings.
# Origins
Fey creatures hail from the two moons of [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]] [[Lux the Moon of Dawn|Lux]] and [[Nox the Moon of Dusk|Nox]], sometimes called the [[The Celestials|Celestial]] Moons. These moons are the home of the [[The Elder Creator Gods|Elder God]] [[Tel]] and his offspring: [[The Celestials]]. Long ago many of the creatures native to these moons crossed the borders that bridge between Eruven and the moons and took residence across the mundane world. These creatures took up residence in the many wodes and natural areas of the world, and are known in modern times as Fey. Depending on the moon of origin, fey creatures have different natural dispositions. Fey native to Lux are typically of a more whimsical, lighthearted, and mischievous nature, and those native to Nox are darker tricksters and potentially bloodthirsty creatures.
# Appearance
Due to the immense variety of the many different fey creatures, there is very little held in common regarding their appearance. On a broad level, most fey creatures incorporate natural elements in their attire and oftentimes sport long pointed ears and other exaugurated features.
# Religion
*For more on this subject, see [[The Fey Courts]]*

Fey [[Religion & Deities|Religion]] is not a distinct entity, however among the [[The Fey Courts|Fey Courts]], fey creatures have deep reverence to the leading Archfey of the courts. Due to their origins on the moons of Lux and Nox, some fey also see the god [[Tel]] as their creator and worship him and his [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|legendary heroes]] accordingly. Many fey creatures who have integrated into the many [[Humans|human]] [[Species & Cultures|cultures]] of Eruven will likely be a follower of one of the many religions dedicated to the [[The Successor Gods|Younger Successor Gods]].
# Homelands
Fey call the [[Elves|elf]]-haunted [[Wodes|wodes]], natural areas, and even some human societies their home.
