Of the [[Elves|elves]], high elves were made in the image of the [[Solis Celestials]], and were created to tend the libraries of the [[The Celestials|Celestials]] and serve as the true elves' heralds. High elf history describes an alleged better age, before the coming of humans and war to the world, when the celestials were still in the world and all that mattered was art and beauty. While not in large numbers, high elves can be found in the many [[Human Realms|human realms]] of [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]].

In the millennia since their creators retired to [[Lux the Moon of Dawn|Lux]], the high elves built a civilization for themselves, primarily living in and among the fallen [[The Celestial Sky Cities|celestial sky cities]]. With no creators left to please, the elves continue as they did before: collecting lore and knowledge and worshiping art. With every generation the high elves turn ever more inward, distrusting outsiders.
# Cultures
In contrast to the many varied human cultures and societies on Eruven, high elf culture is essentially homogenous across the world. This does not mean that there are no political or societal differences at all among the high elves, however it does mean that if you took two elves from different sides of the world and compared them to two humans, the elves would clearly share much in common. High elves believe knowledge is the pursuit from which all good things flow: life, power, legacy. 

High elven societies and cultures are based off of the fallen sky city they inhabit and live around. It is extremely likely that there are at least a few sky cities around the world that have eluded the knowledge of any outsiders, and in fact the location of any of the fallen sky cities is an incredibly closely held secret, due to distrust of outsiders and the risk of treasure hunters looting ancient and powerful celestial artifacts.

# Origins
High elves trace their origins to the Solis Celestials, who created the high elves along with the [[Dusk Celestials]] creating the [[Wode Elves|wode elves]] shortly after the arrival of the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] to Eruven. The celestial made the high elves to be faithful attendants of the celestials’ sky cities. They were librarians, heralds, antiquarians, and artists of all kinds.
# Appearance
Like all other elves, high elves' height can range from 5.5 to 6.5 feet, can weigh from 100 150 pounds, and can expect to live for 400 to 600 years. High elves have tall, willowy bodies, pointed ears, and smoother skin than most other humanoids. Rare metal inlays such as gold, platinum, palladium, and iridium acts as organic components within their skin.

Elven [[Religion & Deities|Religion]], across cultural lines, has its foundations in the worship of their patron god [[Tel]], the celestials, and Tel's [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|legendary heroes]].
# Homelands
High elves may call any [[Wodes|wodes]] their home, as the magical elf-haunted wodes represent Tel’s vision for the world. Specifically, high elves typically call the fallen sky cities of the celestials, which lie hidden deep within Eruven's wodes.
