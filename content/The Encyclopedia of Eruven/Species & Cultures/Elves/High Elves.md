Of the [[Elves|elves]], high elves were made in the image of the [[Solis Celestials]], and were created to be tend the libraries of the [[The Celestials|Celestials]] and serve as the true elves' heralds. High elf history describes an alleged better age, before the coming of humans and war to the world, when the celestials were still in the world and all that mattered was art and beauty. While not in large numbers, high elves can be found in the many [[Human Realms|human realms]] of Eruven.

In the millennia since their creators retired to [[Lux the Moon of Dawn|Lux]], the high elves built a civilization for themselves, primarily living in and among the fallen [[The Celestial Sky Cities|celestial sky cities]]. With no creators left to please, the elves continue as they did before: collecting lore and knowledge and worshiping art. With every generation the high elves turn ever more inward, distrusting outsiders.
