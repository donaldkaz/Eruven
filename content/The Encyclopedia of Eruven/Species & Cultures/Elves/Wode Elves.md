Of the [[Elves|elves]], wode elves were made in the image of the [[Dusk Celestials]], and were created to be the caretakers and guardians of [[Tel|Tel’s]] sacred [[Wodes|wodes]] and all of their inhabitants. 

They are masters of the wodes and see all of [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven’s]] forests as their domain by birthright. They have a reputation among [[Humans|humans]] who wonder too far into the woods, with many wode elves believing that humans *should* fear the woods.
# Cultures
The primary value of wode elf culture is respect and reverence for their home, the wode in which they live. They speak of the wode as though it were alive and conscious, dangerous and delightful. Wode elves are receptive to strangers who respect the land upon which they trespass. However, those who seek to harm the wilds ruin their only chance of treating peacefully within the wode’s beating heart.

Wode elf societies and cultures are based around the wode they inhabit and is ruled by what is known as a court. While there are wodes scattered across all of Eruven, the highest number and density of wodes on the mundane plane is located within the [[Human Realms|human realm]] of [[Corvallis]]. # Origins
Wode elves trace their origins to the Dusk [[The Celestials|Celestials]], who created the wode elves along with the [[Solis Celestials]] creating the high elves shortly after the arrival of the [[Dwarves|dwarves]]. The celestials made the wode elves to be custodians, guardians, and curators of Tel's beloved wodes on Eruven.
# Appearance
Like all others elves, wode elves' height can range from 5.5 to 6.5 feet, can weigh from 100 to 150 pounds, and can expect to live for 400 to 600 years. Wode elves are lithe humanoids with finely furred skin in all shades of blue. They have angular features, large wide-set eyes, and large pointed ears akin to a bat's. They possess a supernatural glamor that allows them to blend in with their surroundings when they wish to hide.

# Homelands
Wode elves call any wode or forest their home, as the magical elf-haunted wodes represent Tel's vision for the world. Specifically, wode elf courts typically rule out of hidden groves deep inside the lush foliage and trees of their wodes.
