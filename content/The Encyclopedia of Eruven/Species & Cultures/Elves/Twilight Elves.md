Of the [[Elves|elves]], twilight elves are unique in the fact that they were made in the image of both the [[Solis Celestials|Solis]] and [[Dusk Celestials|Dusk]] Celestials. They were made to be the servants of the [[The Celestials|Celestials]] on [[Lux the Moon of Dawn]] and [[Nox the Moon of Dusk]]. This was after [[Tel]] and his Celestials left [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]] upon the arrival of [[Humans|humans]] and war to the mundane plane. Twilight elves are extremely rare on Eruven, as they mostly reside on Lux and Nox, however they can occasionally be found in highly populated cities or as envoys or visitors within the courts of [[High Elves|high]] and [[Wode Elves|wode]] elves. 

They are the faithful mortal servants of the Celestials, and have a reputation among elves as being imperious, aloof, and haughty. Because the twilight elves are denizens of the two moons Lux and Nox, they come across as more alien than high and even wode elves. 
# Cultures
Twilight elf culture and society is based around deep reverence to the Celestials. Twilight elf society can be described as a warrior society, as they are often used as strikers for the Celestials when an issue arises on Eruven that requires mortal intervention.
# Origins
Twilight elves trace their origins to the cooperation between the Solis and Dusk celestials, who created the twilight elves after Tel and the Celestials left the mundane world for Tel's private [[The Planar Expanse|planes]] of Lux and Nox. The celestials made the twilight elves to be warriors and stewards for the celestials in their new homes.
# Appearance
Like all other elves, twilight elves' height can range from 5.5 to 6.5 feet, can weigh 100 to 150 pounds, and can expect to live 400 to 600 years. Twilight elves are tall and lithe, sport pointed ears and extremely pale skin and hair. Due to their unique connection to the moons Lux and Nox, twilight elves seem to be lit by an unseen sun. In places like Eruven, they appear washed out, silhouetted, or grayscale.
# Religion
*For more on this subject, see [[Elven Religions]]*

Elven [[Religion & Deities|Religion]], across cultural lines, has its foundations in the worship of their patron god [[Tel]], the celestials, and Tel's [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|legendary heroes]].
# Homelands
Twilight elves call the two moons, Lux and Nox, their home. They live among the elves' legendary heroes and celestials as mortal servants and agents.
