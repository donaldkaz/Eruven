After [[Humans|humans]], smallfolk, also known as Polder, are the most numerous and diverse [[Species & Cultures|ancestry]] in [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]]. They are not humans, but live in and among humans and share their gods and culture. Almost every human culture in Eruven has a polder [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|saint]] or a human saint venerated by polder.

Short, averaging 3.5 feet tall, the polders' origins are obscure. They are a young species who, like humans, have not single patron [[The Gods|god]]. They often enjoy a reputation as excellent spies and thieves thanks to their stature. Many smallfolk consider this a base slander and point out they're also famed as chefs and tinkerers, though polders can be found in every profession, especially in cities.
# Cultures
Smallfolk have no unique cultures of societies of their own, instead they live among humans and share the customs, gods, and ways of life.
# Origins
Similarly to the origins of humanity, there is no clear answer on the origins of the smallfolk. One thing is clear however, there is no reference to the polder in the ancient histories describing the dawn times, implying that unlike the [[Dwarves|dwarves]], [[Elves|elves]], humans, and [[Orcs|orcs]], the smallfolk were not created by the gods as a native species to Eruven.

Due to this lack of reference in much historical record, some scholars posit that the polders are, in fact, a [[Fabricated Humanoids|fabricated species]], similarly to the [[Draconians|draconians']] being a people created by an obscure arcane ritual known as Dracogenisis.
# Appearance
Polders are very short, in accordance with the moniker of smallfolk, with their height ranging from 3 to 4 feet and their weight ranging 20 to 50 pounds. Polders can expect to live 50 to 90 years, a bit shorter than a human.
# Religion
*For more on this subject, see [[The Successor Gods]]*

Similarly to the lack of any unique culture or society, smallfolk ascribe themselves to the [[Religion & Deities|Religion]] of whatever culture or society they were raised in, typically human.
# Homelands
Smallfolk, like humans, are found across the entire plane within human cultures present on every continent of Eruven.
