Of the three other native[[Species & Cultures|peoples]] to [[The Mundane World of Eruven]], the [[Dwarves|dwarves]], [[Elves|elves]], and [[Humans|humans]], orcs were the last to be created and placed on Eruven by their patron [[The Gods|god]] [[Vek]]. 

Orcs are renowned across Eruven as consummate warriors, true masters of warfare and the martial arts. Each orc has within them a fire that causes their veins to glow once blood is drawn. This anger propels them right to the edge of death. The dichotomy between their desire to be left alone and their zeal in battle is summarized in a dwarf proverb: 

"Be thankful orcs do not hold grudges."
# Cultures
Orc history spans the world and all its ages. They are an adaptable people, settling into villages and cities, hot and cold climates, high in the mountains and deep underground. Most orcs live in diverse humanoid societies, though a few live secluded in the tropics or the borderlands between society and the elf-haunted [[Wodes|wodes]]. Orcs are strong people and loyal to a fault. They place great importance in community and being of service to family and friends.

Although many orcs spend their lives in diverse humanoid societies, orcs also form loose mercenary bands and clans in the borderlands, preferring the natural places of the world while avoiding the elf-haunted wodes.

# Origins
Orcs are the children of the [[The Elder Creator Gods|elder god]] [[Vek]], created at during the twilight of creation after the rest of the native humanoid peoples of Eruven had been made. Because of their late coming to the world, orcs don't quite fit in anywhere, instead either integrating into already existing societies or forming warbands, mercenary groups, and clans along the borderlands of society in order to survive.
# Appearance
Orcs are slightly taller, wider, and stronger than humans, with their height ranging from 5.5 to 6.5 feet and weight 150 to 300 pounds. They can expect to live about 50 to 90 years. Orcs have sing in greens tones and a supernatural energy they call "the blood fire" that flows in their veins, igniting colorful lines in an orc's skin during life-or-death moments. Orcs have strong angular facial features and sport hair shades in the same range as human hair.
# Religion
*For more on this subject, see [[The Elder Creator Gods]]*

Orcish [[Religion & Deities|Religion]], unlike other religions, does not revolve around the worship of Vek. Uniquely among the Elder Creator Gods, Vek does not desire worship. Orcs still call out to Vek in desperate times, but in those moments they do not call out for aid, only that Vek witness them, and that they might prove worthy of his attention. Vek's [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|legendary heroes]] are not philosophers or poets, but warriors and hunters.
# Homelands
Outside of the diverse cities that many orcs call home, many orc clans or mercenary bands call the wild borderlands of Eruven home. They live in forests, mountains and hills, gullies, and hidden desert oases.
