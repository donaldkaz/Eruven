The **History & Myths** of [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]] is a tapestry woven from verifiable chronicle and ancestral legend alike. While scholars may distinguish sharply between historical facts and mythological tradition, the peoples of this world often do not. Indeed, in a world where [[The Gods|gods]] demonstrably intervene and ancient magics still echo through the present day, the boundary between history and myth is less certain than one might prefer.

This section endeavors to present both the documented histories of Eruven and the myths that various [[Species & Cultures|cultures]] hold as sacred truth. Where historical records exist, I have consulted primary sources and cross-referenced accounts. Where only legend remains, I have presented the most widely accepted versions while noting that significant variations may exist.

It should be understood that even our most reliable histories grow murky as one travels backward through the ages. The further we retreat from the present, the more fact blends with legend, until at last we arrive at an era where only myth can guide us; the time of creation itself, the age of [[The Elder Creator Gods|Elder Gods]] and first peoples, when the very fabric of the world was still being woven.
