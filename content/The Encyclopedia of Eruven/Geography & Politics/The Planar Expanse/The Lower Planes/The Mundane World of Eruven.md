Eruven, also known as The Mundane World or The Prime [[The Planar Expanse|Manifold]], is named after [[Eruv]], one of the four [[The Elder Creator Gods|Elder Gods]] believed to have created the world. This is the home world of [[Dwarves|dwarves]], [[Elves|elves]], [[Humans|humans]], [[Orcs|orcs]], and many more [[Species & Cultures]].

Eruven is made up of three continents, which can be further broken up into ten major regions: [[Amaran]], [[Angorath]], [[Corvallis]], [[Kharredin]], [[Nephara]], [[Olythia]], [[Tzalco]], [[Virellie]], [[Zal'amar]], and [[Zhaern]]. Collectively these regions are known as [[Human Realms|The Human Realms]], and are broken up along cultural, geographic, and climatic lines.

# Astronomy
Eruven has two moons in the night sky with opposite phases. The two moons are [[Lux the Moon of Dawn]] and [[Nox the Moon of Dusk]], Lux with an amber hue and Nox with an lavender hue. There are thousands stars in the night sky, three significant ones being [[Axiom the Plane of Uttermost Law|Axiom]], [[Primordium the Plane of Uttermost Chaos|Primordium]] and [[Synthesia the Plane of All Elements|Synthesia]].

The Mundane World has quite a few adjacent planes one brave, smart, or foolish enough to attempt to travel to. Firstly, the two moons Lux and Nox are very closely aligned with Eruven, allowing under certain conditions travel between said planes. Additionally, someone my try to travel down through the bowels of our world into [[The World Below]], as the Mundane World is stacked right atop the World Below in the [[The Planar Expanse|Planar Expanse]]. Finally, Eruven is situated not far below the [[The Elemental Planes|Elemental Planes]] and [[Synthesia the Plane of All Elements]] in the Expanse.
