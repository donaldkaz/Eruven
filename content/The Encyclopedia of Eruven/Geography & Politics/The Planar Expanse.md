The observed universe around us is known as the Planar Expanse. [[The Mundane World of Eruven]] is but only one world in the expanse, each star in the night sky is, in fact, a world unto themselves. This fact is not known to most people living on Eruven. While most people on our mundane world use the term "[[Plane|plane]]" to describe these worlds, sages and scholars using geometric formulae describe these worlds as "manifolds".

The expanse itself can be divided into three sections; [[The Higher Planes of Existence]], [[The Lower Planes of Existence]], and [[The Astral Sea]] in the space in-between the many planes of existence. The highest of the planes are the planes of [[Axiom the Plane of Uttermost Law]] and [[Primordium the Plane of Uttermost Chaos]]. The lowest plane of existence is [[The Abyssal Waste]].
