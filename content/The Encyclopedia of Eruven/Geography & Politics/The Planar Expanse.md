The observed universe around us is known as the Planar Expanse. [[The Mundane World of Eruven]] is but only one world in the expanse. Each star in the night sky is, in fact, a world unto themselves. This fact is not known to most people living on Eruven. While most people on our mundane world use the term "plane" to describe these worlds, sages and scholars using geometric formulae describe these worlds as "manifolds".

The expanse itself contains worlds with [[The Astral Sea]] between them, and can be divided into two different categories; [[The Higher Planes of Existence]] and [[The Lower Planes of Existence]]. The worlds are defined by how "energetic" they are, with higher worlds being more energetic. The highest of the planes are the planes of [[Axiom the Plane of Uttermost Law]] and [[Primordium the Plane of Uttermost Chaos]] and the lowest plane of existence is [[The Abyssal Waste]] with every other world either being more or less energetic.

# Traversing the Planes
The space between all worlds of the Expanse is known as the [[The Astral Sea|Astral Sea]]. It connects all worlds, and its geometry varies between different parts of the Expanse, sometimes behaving as a liquid ocean and at other times acting as a weightless void. In addition to any of the arcane or highly technological methods of traversing the Astral Sea, one can also traverse the planes where they may overlap, in areas where the barrier between the two worlds is weakened. 

For example, if one delves deep enough into the earth or enters the correct cave, such a traveler may find themselves stumbling into [[The World Below]]. In fact, if the traveler continues even deeper through the cave networks of The World Below, they may find themselves entering the barren wastes of [[The Seven Cities of Hell|Hell]]. Finally, if that were not enough, said traveler could continue deeper down through Hell and into the lowest of worlds: [[The Abyssal Waste]].
