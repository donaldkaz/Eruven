[[The Mundane World of Eruven]] is a realm of vast continents, treacherous seas, and lands both civilized and wild. Within these territories mortals have forged kingdoms and empires, each shaped by the geography they inhabit and the ambitions of those who lead them. **Geography & Politics** examines the physical world itself, the political entities that have risen upon it, and the significant individuals whose actions have shaped the course of nations.

It must be acknowledged that the political landscape of Eruven is ever-shifting. Borders advance and recede with conquest and treaty and rulers rise and fall with alarming frequency. What is recorded here represents the most current and reliable information available at the time of this writing.
# The Mundane World
We shall begin with an examination of Eruven itself: the geography of the Mundane World, its continents, seas, and natural features that define them.
- [[The Mundane World of Eruven]]

Following our survey of the physical realm, we shall turn out attention to the organized societies that govern its [[Species & Cultures|peoples]]. These political entities vary dramatically in structure, philosophy, and stability, yet each exerts profound influence over the lives of those within their borders. Due to their outsized significance in comparison to the other peoples of Eruven, we will focus on the societies of the [[Humans|humans]] spread across all manner of biomes, the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] who reside in the depths of Eruven's mountains, and the [[Elves|elves]] who reside in the magical [[Wodes|wodes]].

# The Planar Expanse
Finally, we must acknowledge that our Mundane World is but one plane among many. Beyond the familiar horizons of Eruven lie other realms of existence, some connected to our world through planar convergence and overlap, others accessible only through arcane means or divine intervention. Understanding these planes is essential for comprehending the full scope of reality and the forces that influence mortal affairs.
