# The Mundane World
To begin, we will examine the nature of our our Mundane World of [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]]. Additionally, we will go over the [[Human Realms|Ten Realms]] we divide our world into to better understand the politics and place in the world of [[Humans|humanity]].

Next, we will look at the [[Dwarven Societies|societies]] of the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] oftentimes found deep within the mountains of our world.

The [[Elves]] are another significant people who live in the world, and their societies reside in the ancient magical [[Wodes|wodes]] that blanket large swathes of the surface or Eruven.
