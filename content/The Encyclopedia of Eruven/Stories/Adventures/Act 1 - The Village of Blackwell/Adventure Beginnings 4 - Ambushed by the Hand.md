## Introduction
### Session Description
Solving issues and stealing stones. Broadening horizons.
### Opening Monologue
> Every new age is defined by the one that came before.
> 
> This is an age of Chaos -- a lawless land fractured by the betrayal of King Aldrin the Just, struck down by Thalric, the King Beneath the Umbral Sun. 
> 
> Hope flickers. Order crumbles. This is a land in need of heroes. 
> 
> It was the 12th of May, Year 33, when seven adventurers met with [[Dunquat]] the stone mason of the village of [[The Village of Blackwell]]. They discovered that the dwarven stone merchants of [[Val Kalavar]] were refusing to deliver the stones Dunquat ordered unless Dunquat paid his quantum satchel in addition to the already agreed upon payment for the stones. The stones were being held under lock and key in the village's stable house.
> Then, the party visited [[Mikael]] the village carpenter, who was attacked by a huge wolf in the south woods while he was cutting wood because the woodcutter [[Vaughn]] refuses to go into the northern woods because he is afraid of bandits camped in the forest.
> Following along the river south east out of Blackwell, the party eventually came upon an itinerant shepherd named [[Yerris]], standing shocked and panicing outside of a wolf den. Yerris and his brother [[Oleq]] were ambushed as they were passing the den by a large wolf, which tore through quite a few sheep and grabbed Oleq, dragging him into the den. Going into the cave, there was a rushing underground river infested with elemental creatures, and deeper inside the heroes battled a werewolf who was trying to turn Oleq. After a few close calls the heroes defeated the beast and saved Oleq, salvaging some of the werewolf's remains in order to try and create a cure for the carpenter Mikael.
> We begin again, after short ten minute rest outside of the werewolf's den on the banks of the [[The Dunn River|Dunn River]], the two shepherd brothers recollecting their scattered flock in preparation to leave this troubled land.

## Gilded Hand Ambush
The party, once they get to the town square to head to bed, are ambushed by the [[Gilded Hand]]. They plan to abduct [[Nyxarkit'ya Kit-Kat]] and abandon their plan of finding the [[The Cup of Iulius]]. They have a black and white bounty sheet describing the capture of Kit-Kat in exchange for a large bounty, they should deliver her to the massive capital city [[Nesso]] of [[Virellie]] to someone under the alias of ***The Scribe***
### Entering the Square
> Passing through the village square towards the [[Feather Bed Inn]] for the night, the surrounding environment is eerily quiet. The only illumination in this large square is shed by the windows of the buildings surrounding you and the lights you carry.
> Three members of the Gilded Hand emerge from the shadows, weapons drawn. There is the older human woman wizard, the shadowy halfling man, and the squat dwarf man. The woman steps forward, holding out a sheet of parchment, "We have a poster here that calls for the head of your friend there," as she point to Kit-Kat, "You have two options, either you hand her over peacefully, or we do things the hard way, and we can't promise you won't get hurt."

Combat encounter with stats from Flee Mortals pages 352-353: **Jedar Pike**, **Mycete**, and **The Horror**.
### Aftermath
The gilded hand will send people down **non-lethally** unless the party kills or sends down one of their own. They will try to take *Kit-Kat* down **non-lethally** primarily. If the party surrenders Kit-Kat or fights to defend Kit-Kat and lose, the Gilded Hand will give over the Delian Knight Commander's Ring as "payment". If the party is victorious the captured Gilded Members will share everything they know about the plot hoping that the heroes will show mercy:
- The party is in possession of an artifact, a Delian Knight Commander's Ring which can unlock the third level of the tomb to access the Cup of Iulius.
- They met [[Queen Bargnot]], who promised a huge amount for the cup, a deal the Gilded Hand took.
- They reveal that the [[Jagged Edge]] is camped out in the ruins of Castle Andreas.
