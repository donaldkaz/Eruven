### Opening Monologue
> Every new age is defined by the one that came before.
> 
> This is an age of Chaos -- a lawless land fractured by the betrayal of King Aldrin the Just, struck down by Thalric, the King Beneath the Umbral Sun. 
> 
> Hope flickers. Order crumbles. This is a land in need of heroes. 
> 
> It was the 11th of May, Year 33, when seven adventurers returned to [[The Village of Blackwell]] triumphant with a thankful blacksmith Ashleigh and her rescued daughter Violet in tow. Violet had been captured by agents of Queen Bargnot, goblin leader of a group of traveling brigands called the Jagged Edge. They had been driven off by the village of Blackwell a few years ago and, for some reason, have come back to the area for vengeance.
> Upon returning to town and parting with Ashleigh and Violet the party were met by the elderly Abbott Loric of the local Church of [[Saint Gryffyn the Stout]]. He beseeched the heroes to help the many villagers in need of help, and promised to begin research into the Delian Tomb so that whatever the Jagged Edge is searching for can be found by the heroes first.
