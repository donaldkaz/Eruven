The Elder Gods constitute the oldest known pantheon within the [[The Planar Expanse|Planar Expanse]]. According to the most widely accepted scholarly traditions, every major intelligent ancestry native to the [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Mundane World]], primarily the [[The Elder Dragons| Elder Dragons]], [[Dwarves|dwarves]], [[Elves|elves]], [[Humans|humans]], and [[Orcs|orcs]], was originally created by one or more of these ancient deities.

Following the creation of the orcs, the practice of crafting new mortal peoples diminished. Some theologians attribute this cessation to the turbulent era traditionally associated with the rise of humankind, as recorded in the most prominent version of the [[Creation Myth|Eruven Creation Myth]].

In accordance with the [[The Law of Nature|Law of Nature]], the Elder Gods, like all deities, are prohibited from direct interference in the world, relying instead on intermediaries such as [[Saints & Legendary Heroes| saints, heralds, and legendary heroes]] to enact their will.
# Creation
The Elder Gods are responsible for writing the [[The Laws of Creation|Laws of Creation]], establishing the current organization of the [[The Higher Planes of Existence|Higher]] and [[The Lower Planes of Existence|Lower]] planes of existence, and bringing the world of Eruven itself into being. Their actions shaped the fundamental principles by which magic, life, death, and natural order continue to operate.
# Members
The number of deities known as the Elder Gods is relatively short, and are listed below:
- [[Dus]] the Tidemother, creator of the seas and rains.
- [[Eruv]] the Maker, creator of land, mountains, and stone and patron of the dwarves.
- [[Kuv]] the Destroyed, mythic creator of humanity.
- [[Neph]] the Architect of Magic, author of the [[The Law of Magic|Law of Magic]] and creator of the Elder Dragons.
- [[Pal]] the Dead God, the late god of Death and creator of [[The Law of Death]]
- [[Ren]] the First Green, creator of life and the author of the [[The Law of Nature|Law of Nature]].
- [[Tel]] the Noble Lord, patron of the elves.
- [[Tul]] the Whispering Gale, creator of the air, clouds, and winds of the world.
- [[Vek]] Father of Flames, creator of fire, volcanoes, and magma and patron of the orcs.
