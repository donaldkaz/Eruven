Like all the [[The Successor Gods|Successor Gods]], the gods of [[Corvallis]] embody the attitudes of the people who live in that region. This includes the [[Humans|humans]], [[Smallfolk|polder]], and [[Draconians|draconians]] of Corvallis.

Corvallis is a polytheistic feudal culture. Its people have many superstitions and prejudices, some of which are reflected in the teachings of their gods. [[Adûn]], for instance, teaches that hard physical labor is a moral good and people who work hard every day are honest.

Like all [[Species & Cultures|peoples]] of [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]], the people of Corvallis are well aware there are other gods. They do not particularly think their gods are *better*, just, that they are the local ones that matter to them. A priest of [[Cavall]] who journeyed far from [[Veylan]] and found themselves in the distant desert land of [[Nephara]] could still act as a conduit of [[Saint Llewellyn the Valliant]].
