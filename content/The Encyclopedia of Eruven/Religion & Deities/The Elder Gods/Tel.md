**Domains:** *Creation, Knowledge, Life, Nature, Protection*

Tel, the Noble Lord, First Among Equals, is the patron of the [[Elves]]. He created the [[Celestials]] at the dawn of creation, who in turn created the younger elves: the [[High Elves|High]], [[Twilight Elves|Twilight]], and [[Wode Elves|Wode]] elves.

Tel holds that the greatest purpose a thinking being can commit themselves to is the creation of art and the appreciation of beauty. He keeps the magical, elf-haunted forests called [[Wodes]] close to his heart because they represent his vision for [[The Mundane World of Eruven|Eruven]]. What the world could have been. His growing disgust with the concept of war led him to leave Eruven and take up residence in his private [[Plane|Planes]]: [[Solis]] and [[Noctis]], where all elves hope to someday join their patron.
