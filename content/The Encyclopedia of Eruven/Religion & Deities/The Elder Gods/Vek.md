**Domains:** *Knowledge, Life, Sun, Trickery, War*

Vek, Father of Flames, Lord of the Forge, the Cleansing Fire who put fire and magma within the world at its creation. Vek is the patron of the [[Orcs]]. Vek, with his brother [[Eruv]], created the [[Elementals]] on Eruven in response to the creation of the [[The Elder Dragons|Elder Dragons]] by [[Neph]] and the [[The Celestials]] by [[Tel]]. These elementals were the origins to the [[Dwarves|dwarves]] and orcs, the first and fourth humanoid peoples of Eruven respectively.
# Followers
Vek's fire is the fire that destroys, but it is also the fire that creates, the fire of the forge, the fire that makes meat safe to eat, the fire that cauterizes wounds.

Uniquely among the [[The Elder Creator Gods]], Vek does not desire worship, he finds it distasteful. Orcs still call out to Vek in desperate times as "Vek Who Once Spoke." But in those moments, they do not call out for aid, only that Vek witness them, and that they might prove worthy of his attention.
