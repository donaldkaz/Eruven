The following legendary [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|heroes]] of the [[Elves|elves]] are once-mortal heroes who now dwell among the [[The Celestials|Celestials]] in [[Lux]] or [[Nox]] and make up [[Tel]]'s court. They are venerated by many elves and others who worship Tel.

- [[A Sea of Suns]], also known as the Composer, credited with inventing harmony.
- [[The Taste of Morning]], also known as the Librarian, credited with building the first library.
- [[Ripples of Honey on a Shore of Gold]], also known as Warkiller, she was a scholar, sage, and soldier.
- [[Yllin Dyrvis]], also known as the Beast Heart, guardian of faeries, dryads, and other woodland creatures.
- [[Thyll Hylacae]], also known as Apotharchon, first to learn the language of the trees.
- [[Illwyv li Orchiax]], also known as Manslayer, was the first [[Wode Elves|Wode Elf]] to take up arms to protect the [[Wodes]] from [[Humans|humans]].
- [[Every Strike of Lightning a Lover Betrayed]], also known as the Queen of Twilight, the first [[Shadow Elves|shadow elf]] to be created from the united effort of a [[Solis Celestial|Solis]] and [[Dusk Celestial|Dusk]] celestial.
