The following [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|heroes]] are venerated by many [[Dwarves|dwarves]] and others who follow [[Eruv]].

- [[Zarok the Law-Giver]], the first great hero of the dwarves.
- [[Valak-koth the Seeker]], the Delver, prophet of Eruv.
- [[Stakros the Engineer]], the Machine Mind, founder of the [[The Order of Fabrication|Order of Fabrication]].
