Vek teaches that action is the defining characteristic of being. [[Vek|Vek's]] [[Saints & Legendary Heroes|heroes]] are not philosophers or poets, but warriors and hunters. The following heroes are venerated by many orcs and others who follow Vek.

- [[Khorvath Who Slew a Thousand]], legendary warleader of a mythic band of orcish [[Bloodrunners|bloodrunners]].
- [[Grole the One-Handed]], slayer of a horde of deathless and their master, the living saint [[Saint Morath of Many Tendrills|Morlath of Many Tendrils]].
- [[Khravila Who Ran Forty Leagues]], mythic trickster, who found peace in the face of impending war against all odds.
